Team task-bonus progress

Every team can earn up to 20 points. Points build from the first eligible completed task: 50% of the combined team goal earns 10 points; 100% earns 20. No individual goal must be reached first, and work above 100% adds no extra bonus. Bonus = 20 × the share of the team goal completed, capped at 20, added after the sales adjustment.

How the game works

First sale: 10 base points to the team with the earliest verified Closed Won timestamp within each matchup. No sale means no bonus. Exact timestamp ties split the bonus between teams.

Same-day close: created and first entered Closed Won on the same Pacific calendar day. Each opportunity can earn this once.

Team-size adjustment: sales base points × 2 ÷ number of scoring captains, Premium and AE staff. This includes first-sale and same-day sales bonuses. Service staff stay outside the divisor. Add the team task bonus after this adjustment. Raw COI and activity stay unchanged.

Weekly results: sales score and task bonus are each rounded to two decimals, then added; equal total scores tie after the week closes. In-progress scores do not create W/L/T records. Points ranks show current totals.

What counts in this snapshot

Revenue is Total COI for opportunities first won during the period. Current COI values are used; subsequent amount edits are not reconstructed. Opportunities now reopened or lost are excluded. Re-closes of earlier wins do not earn a new sale.

Calls are completed Phone Call tasks dated by their Completed Date/Time in Pacific time. Meetings are completed Experience Center, Virtual, Off-Site and In-Person events, dated by their Pacific start time. These call and meeting categories cover all eight captains and 19 sales reps. Service teammates receive task-bonus credit only; the same task never earns points in both categories.

Refreshes hourly, Monday-Friday, 8 a.m.-5 p.m. Pacific. Each Tuesday at 5:01 p.m. Pacific starts the next scoring week. A rollover refresh runs at that time. Future weeks have no stats yet. The sales component still reflects differences in deal size and territory.
